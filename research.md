## Original shortlist

- **[Boon Nam](https://www.boonnam.fi/menu-eng)** — modern Thai at Hotel St. George, family-style sharing. Official hours list Monday–Saturday: 21 and 23 fit. The current linked PDF's prices could not be reliably extracted; the site asks for budget confirmation instead of presenting an invented total. [Official story](https://www.boonnam.fi/our-story), [city tourism listing and Instagram](https://tpr.hel.fi/TPRalusta/en/notification/info/4156/).
- **[Flor](https://www.restaurantflor.com/)** — five-course surprise menu €49/person, €98 for two. Wednesday–Sunday: 21 and 22 fit. Its website calls Flor Natura's little sister and describes seasonal cooking and organic/biodynamic wines.
- **[Shelter](https://www.shelter.fi/menu-en)** — Menu Shelter €64/person, €128 for two. User restriction: **21 or 23 only**. The official [home page](https://www.shelter.fi/) says it has operated in a Kanavaranta warehouse since 2016 and lists Monday–Saturday opening.
- **[Aoi](https://www.restaurantaoi.com/)** — contemporary craft cooking. Thursday–Sunday, with Sunday 14–21: 21 and 22 fit. Its seasonal menu changes every six weeks. The current linked PDF could not be reliably extracted, so no price is fabricated. This is Helsinki's Aoi, not the similarly named Japanese restaurant outside Finland.
- **[Farouge](https://www.farouge.fi/wp-content/uploads/2026/08/Farouge-menu-September-2026.pdf)** — classic set mezze €53/person, minimum two; €106 for two, or €134 with two €14 desserts, before drinks. User restriction: **21 only**. The official menu explains the name and family-feast story. [Opening hours](https://www.farouge.fi/en/restaurant-farouge/).
- **[Farang](https://www.farang.fi/menu/illallinen/a-la-carte)** — user restriction: **21 or 23 only**. Example using full-size shared dishes: crispy pork €32 + chicken curry €32 + papaya salad €26 + two rice €12 + two €12 desserts = €126, before drinks. Menu explains its sharing format. [City tourism listing and Instagram](https://tpr.hel.fi/TPRalusta/en/notification/info/11272/).
- **[Finnjävel Sali](https://finnjavel.fi/en/sali/)** — casual Sali, distinct from Salonki. Dinner menu €65/person, €130 for two. User restriction: **21 only**. Official venue description locates it in the Kunsthalle exhibition building. Its website also promotes Salonki reservations; choose the Sali booking flow.
- **[La Galleria](https://www.lagalleria.fi/)** — Italian at Unioninkatu 30. [Published listing](https://restaurantguru.com/La-Galleria-Helsinki) shows all three days open and links @lagalleriahelsinki. Official website was unavailable to the research browser; current budget and hours need confirmation. No exact current meal total is asserted.
